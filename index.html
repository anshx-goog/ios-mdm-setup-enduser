Executive Mobile Briefing • iOS

Setting Up [Insert Organization Name] Email & Google Apps on Your iPhone

Before your iPhone opens your work Gmail, Calendar, or Google Drive, Apple requires a one-time workplace security pass (called a Profile). Follow the six visual steps below—the setup takes about three minutes.

1

Turn on your work account inside Gmail

Open the Gmail app on your iPhone.

  • If you only use Gmail for work: Sign in with [Insert Work Email Address]. If a dark box titled Device Policy Alert pops up, tap NEXT.
  • If your personal Gmail is already open on your phone: Tap your circular profile photo or initial in the top-right corner of Gmail, then tap Manage accounts on this device.
  • Find your official work email address and tap the slider next to Use in Gmail so it turns blue (ON). (If your work email is not listed yet, tap + Add another account first.)
  • Tap Done in the top-right corner.
Switch Use in Gmail ON, then tap Done
2

Open "Google Device Policy" and tap the blue arrow

As soon as you switch on your work email, Gmail will ask for the Google Device Policy app:

  1. A white box will pop up over Gmail saying "Install the latest version of Google Device Policy so you can access your work data." Tap APP STORE.
  2. In the App Store, tap Get (if installing for the first time) or Open (if it is already on your iPhone).
  3. Inside the Install profile screen, tap the round blue circle with the white downward arrow on the right side. (If Google briefly asks you to confirm your account, tap Continue.)
2A. Tap APP STORE, then tap Open
2B. Tap the round blue downward arrow
3

Download the security pass in Safari

Apple only permits iPhone security passes to download inside Safari (Apple's built-in compass browser). Depending on your phone's browser setup, follow Path A or Path B:

Path A — If Safari opens on its own and asks "Do you want to allow this?":

Tap Allow, tap Close on the Profile Downloaded message, and go straight to Step 4. (Do not tap the blue "Download profile" button again once the pop-up has appeared.)

Path B — If you see a white screen saying "Copy the link from the URL bar to the Safari app":

Your phone opened the link inside Google Chrome instead of Safari. Move it to Safari in three quick taps:

  1. Copy the link: Press and hold your finger for two seconds on the top address pill (ios-mdm.googleusercontent.com), then tap Copy link.
  2. Paste into Safari's bottom address bar: Open Safari (the blue compass icon on your Home Screen). Tap the Search or enter website name bar at the very bottom of the screen—not the Google search box in the middle of the page. Tap again, tap Paste (or Paste and Go), and press the blue arrow (→) key on your keyboard.
  3. Tap Download & Allow: When Installing your profile opens in Safari, tap the blue Download profile button once, tap Allow, and tap Close.
3A. Hold top bar & tap Copy link
3B. Paste in Safari's bottom bar
3C. Tap Allow, then tap Close
4

Locate the pass inside your iPhone Settings

Swipe up to leave Safari and tap the grey Settings gear icon on your iPhone Home Screen:

  • Shortcut at the top (4A): Look right underneath your name and Apple ID banner near the top of Settings and tap Profile Downloaded.
  • If "Profile Downloaded" is not at the top (4B): Scroll down slightly in Settings, tap General, tap VPN & Device Management, and tap Google Workspace device policy payload profile under Downloaded Profile.
4A. Top of Settings: Profile Downloaded
4B. Or via General → VPN & Device Management
5

Enter your iPhone PIN and approve Apple's warning

  1. On the Install Profile screen, tap the blue Install button in the top-right corner.
  2. Type your 6-digit iPhone unlock passcode (the number code you use to unlock your phone—Apple does not use Face ID on this screen). If IT is assisting you on a call, pause screen sharing briefly while typing your PIN.
  3. A Warning screen will appear stating that an administrator can collect data or remotely erase your iPhone. Apple displays this exact same generic legal text to every company worldwide; in practice, this pass simply lets [Insert Organization Name] protect company mail and clear work data if a phone is ever lost. Tap Install in the top-right corner.
  4. A small box titled Remote Management will pop up with Trust printed in red letters. Even though red normally signals a stop button, Trust is Apple's confirmation button here. Tap Trust.
  5. Wait two seconds until the top reads Profile Installed, then tap the blue checkmark (✓) in the top-right corner.
5A. Tap Install & enter iPhone PIN
5B. Tap Install, then red Trust
5C. Tap the blue checkmark (✓)
6

Quick IT approval & opening your work inbox

As a final safeguard, your work inbox remains locked behind the scenes until IT approves your handset in the company console.

  1. Notify IT for instant approval: Message or call [Insert Named IT Contact & Phone/WhatsApp]. If you open Gmail before IT clicks approve, you will see a notice stating "Your administrator must approve this device"—that confirms your installation succeeded.
  2. Open your work inbox: Once IT confirms approval (typically under two minutes), open Gmail. If your personal inbox is still displayed, tap your top-right profile circle → Manage accounts on this device, verify that Use in Gmail is switched ON (blue) under your official ID, and tap Done.
What changes on your phone once active: Your official email, Calendar, and Drive work as normal, while copying work text into personal apps or sharing company attachments out to WhatsApp or personal Gmail is restricted automatically to protect company data.
